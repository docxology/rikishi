### Verification and presentation

The released implementation retains real-file and subprocess controls, exact
source/artifact bindings, two branch-inclusive coverage floors, bounded Office
projections, actual embedded-image comparisons and complete visual inspection.
The native mutation fixture uses create-only APFS clones on supported macOS
filesystems and an explicit ordinary-copy path elsewhere, without silent fallback.

Actual pass counts, platform observations, artifact hashes and independent
verdicts are in the attached release verification summary. Those results qualify
the tagged 1.0.0 source; changed candidates require their own checks.

### Limits

Finite calculations and named formal statements do not establish general SUMO
consistency, universal semantic preservation, independent proof authentication
or empirical/biological validity. Native platform, physical-device, accessibility
and publication limits remain explicit in the accompanying review record.
